# Semantic interpretation

The portfolio computes classifications independently of rendering. Each state carries a metric, value and unit, reference where available, category, readable label, tone, supporting fields, evidence sufficiency, provenance and limitations. The text takeaways use deterministic templates over those states. Colours supplement the labels.

| Metric | Demonstration defaults | Requirements and source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Readiness | 0–39 low, 40–59 reduced, 60–79 moderate, 80–100 high | Valid score and all four underlying components. The existing fictional weighted calculation is preserved. |
| Recent load | Ratio below 0.80 lighter, 0.80–1.20 typical, above 1.20 through 1.50 elevated, above 1.50 substantially elevated | Last seven completed days of session-RPE load divided by the weekly equivalent of the preceding 28 days. The windows do not overlap. |
| Relative pace | At −5% or lower substantially faster, between −5% and −2% faster, within ±2% near median, between +2% and +5% slower, at +5% or higher substantially slower | Signed change in seconds per kilometre against the peer median. Separate similar-distance and same-route cohorts, at least five peers each. The selected run is excluded. |
| Climb demand | Below the 25th percentile low, 25th–75th typical, above 75th through 90th high, above 90th exceptional | Gain in metres per kilometre compared with at least five same-sport peers. Use the existing distance bands for running and ±15% or 5 km, whichever is wider, for cycling. Rank counts lower peers and half of ties. The raw ascent and strongest 500 m climb remain visible when ride history is sparse. |
| Thermal context | Below 10°C cool, 10 to below 20°C mild, 20 to below 25°C warm, 25 to below 30°C elevated, 30°C or higher high | Uses historical air-temperature estimates or a labelled fictional fixture. FIT device temperature is kept separate. The integration publishes no meteorological heat index. |
| Wind demand | Below 2 m/s limited, 2 to below 4 m/s noticeable, 4 m/s or more strong | The larger of distance-weighted mean positive headwind and mean absolute crosswind. Both components need valid coverage over at least 80% of the route. A constant projected archive vector is explicitly identified as a reference. The bins retain the signed component data. |
| Fitness and consistency | Benchmark time changes beyond ±2% and VO₂max changes beyond ±1 mL/kg/min receive a directional trend. Consistency changes by more than one active week per four receive a directional trend. | Latest two tests versus the preceding two. Consistency compares the median of the last two four-week blocks with the median of the two before them. Each window needs two observations. |
| Recovery inputs | HRV and resting heart rate within 2 ms and 2 bpm, respectively, are stable. Sleep within 30 minutes is stable. Values outside the bands are described as above or below the personal baseline. | Latest seven mornings versus the prior 28, using medians. An above-baseline resting heart rate or below-baseline HRV or sleep is a caution signal. The recent period needs five valid mornings and the prior period needs 14. |
| Workout style | Faster by more than 2% with HR at least 6 bpm higher suggests higher intensity. At least 1.5× typical distance and 60 minutes with HR less than 6 bpm above comparable efforts suggests long endurance. Recovery-style needs at least 5% slower pace, HR at least 7 bpm lower, and ascent at or below its median and within low or typical climb demand. Easy aerobic needs pace at most 2% faster than the median plus HR at least 4 bpm lower. Steady needs pace within 5% and HR within 4 bpm. Other cases are unclassified. | At least five comparable peers with valid timer pace and mean heart rate. The selected activity and peers need at least 80% heart-rate time coverage. These rules use session averages. Workout intent and heart-rate zones are unknown. |

Green or teal indicates favourable evidence, blue or slate describes a typical or stable state, amber indicates higher demand or a caution signal, red marks low readiness, and grey marks insufficient evidence. The demand categories are descriptive demonstration bands. They carry no clinical risk estimate. A single activity cannot establish a fitness trend, and a fast same-route pace with similar heart rate identifies stronger performance at comparable recorded effort for that activity.

The Planner uses the same nonoverlapping load reference for its volume guard. The guard triggers below 72 readiness or above 1.20× reference load. It starts the proposed distance budgets 20% lower and omits the paced session. Lighter applies a further 25% scale before rounding. The illustrative readiness score itself still uses its original overlapping 28-day load window. [Readiness calculation](health-methods.md)
